What is MARVEL Tōkon: Fighting Souls?

MARVEL Tōkon: Fighting Souls is a 4v4 team-based fighting game developed by Arc System Works in partnership with Marvel Games and PlayStation Studios. Players assemble squads of iconic Marvel heroes and villains for fast-paced tag battles.

Featuring anime-inspired visuals, simplified controls for newcomers and deep mechanics for competitive players, Tōkon combines accessible gameplay with high-level strategic depth.
