Wolverine Move List & Playstyle

Wolverine is pure rushdown: relentless claw pressure, Berserker Rage empowerment and fast strike sequences. An ideal Point character for players who want to live in the opponent's face.

Role: Rushdown Team: Unbreakable X-Men Tier: A (v1.0)

Gameplan: get in with X-Force or a Berserker Dive, then chain Berserker Claw strings. Manage Berserker Rage — activate it to extend pressure, or spend it with a finisher when you have a clean hit.

Best Partners for Wolverine

As a rushdown Point, Wolverine wants assists that cover his approach and an Anchor that can close out rounds.

  • Doctor Doom (Assist): Nullify Shield assist lets Wolverine walk forward behind a wall.
  • Storm (Assist): projectile assist covers jump-in angles.
  • Magik (Anchor): strong closer if Wolverine's rush gets shut down.
