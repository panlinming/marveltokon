Iron Man Combos & Builds

Iron Man owns the air and the full screen: Repulsor spam, Unibeam punishes and Free Flight mobility make him one of the strongest zone-control fighters at launch.

Role: Zoning Team: Fighting Avengers Tier: A (v1.0)

Gameplan: keep repulsors flowing to force the opponent to approach on your terms. When they commit to a jump, Unibeam them out of the air — then take the round with Free Flight pressure.

Iron Man Team Builds

Iron Man commonly plays as an Assist provider or a mid-range Point paired with a strong dive character.

  • With Spider-Man: repulsor assist covers Spider-Man's Web Zip approaches — a top launch pairing.
  • With Wolverine: Iron Man lockdown lets Wolverine's X-Force approach safely.
  • As Assist: slot Iron Man behind a rushdown Point to provide Repulsor Shot screen control on call.
