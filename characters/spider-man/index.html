Spider-Man Move List & Combos

Spider-Man is a top-tier rushdown fighter built around Web Zip mobility. Control space with web projectiles, punish with Spidey-Sense counters and open opponents up with Arachnid Assault mix-ups.

Role: Rushdown Team: Amazing Guardians Tier: S (v1.0)
Official character guide: “Master Web Zip to move efficiently, use powerful follow-ups, and trip up opponents with ease!”

Basic Damage↓M » M » H » ↓H » JC » j.H ×2Fundamental ground-to-air string. Jump Cancel (JC) into two aerial Heavies for reliable damage.

Best Partners for Spider-Man

As a Point character, Spider-Man thrives when his team covers his weak neutral against heavy zoners.

  • Doctor Doom (Assist): Nullify Shield and Auric Ray cover Spider-Man's approach against projectile teams.
  • Iron Man (Assist): Repulsor Shot lockdown creates the space needed for Web Hang mix-ups.
  • Magik (Anchor): strong counter-pick backup if Spider-Man loses the lead.
