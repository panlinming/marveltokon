Doctor Doom Combo Guide & Move List

Doctor Doom rules mid-to-long range with the Nullify Shield barrier, then cashes out with Diplomacy command-throw mixes. One of the strongest launch fighters for players who like controlling the screen.

Role: Zoning Team: Knights of Doom Tier: S (v1.0)

Core Moves

MoveInputNotes
Nullify Shield5U (Hold to charge)Doom's signature tool: an energy barrier that absorbs projectiles and also deals damage. Holding charges a stronger version; it can also be placed farther forward to claim space.
Auric RaySkillLong-range beam for screen control and anti-approach.
Mystic CageSkillTrap setup that limits the opponent's movement options; leads into throw mix-ups.
DiplomacyCommand ThrowCommand grab that punishes opponents trying to block or jump through Doom's zoning.
Merciful SmiteSkillClose-range strike to punish jump-ins that get past the shield.

High-Damage Combo Routes

ComboSequenceNotes
Basic↓M » M » H » ↓H » JC » j.HReliable bread-and-butter into Jump Cancel and an aerial finisher.
Nullify Shield ComboForward U » M ×3Place the shield forward, then punish the opponent's reaction with a Medium string.
Mystic Cage into DiplomacyForward QS+M » Back QSCage trap into Quick Skill mix — sets up the Diplomacy command-throw guess.
Complex Route↓M » M » M » Forward QS+M » Back QS+M » H ×2Advanced side-switching route that ends in a heavy knockdown for oki.
Gameplan: use Nullify Shield to absorb the opponent's approach, then force a guess — block the Auric Ray or eat Diplomacy. That strike/throw mix is where Doom wins rounds.

Building a Team Around Doom

Doom works best as a mid or Anchor: he wants gauge and time to set up shields and traps.

  • Spider-Man (Point): forces approaches that Doom can punish with placed Nullify Shields.
  • Iron Man (Assist): Repulsor Shot assist doubles up Doom's screen control.
  • Hulk (Assist): armored assist covers Doom's weaker close-range defense.
