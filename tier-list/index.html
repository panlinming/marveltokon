Why These Characters Lead the Meta

Quick strength and weakness notes for the current top picks.

Doctor Doom

Strengths: Nullify Shield absorbs projectiles while claiming space; Diplomacy command throw opens up blocking opponents.
Weaknesses: weaker when rushed down before shield setups.

Magik

Strengths: elite movement and counter tools make her hard to pin down; strong in the Point slot.
Weaknesses: execution-heavy routes to maximize damage.

Blade

Strengths: consistent pressure and reliable Assist value for team builds.
Weaknesses: fewer flashy comeback tools than other S-tiers.

Spider-Man

Strengths: Web Zip mobility plus Spidey-Sense counters create constant mix-up pressure.
Weaknesses: can struggle into dense projectile zoning.
