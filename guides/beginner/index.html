Beginner Guide & Core Mechanics

MARVEL Tōkon: Fighting Souls reinvents tag fighting around a 4v4 squad and one shared health bar. This guide covers everything a new player needs in the first hours: the Assemble system, basic controls, and the mechanics that decide rounds.

4v4 Team Battles Shared Health Bar PS5 & PC · Crossplay

How 4v4 Assemble Works

The core twist: your whole squad shares one health bar, and not everyone fights from the start.

  • Shared health bar: all four fighters draw from the same health pool — chip damage and round control matter more than individual comeback mechanics.
  • Start with 2 fighters: matches open with only two active squad members. Your 3rd and 4th fighters unlock through Wall Breaks or by surviving round transitions.
  • Assemble Gauge: one team-wide resource that powers four actions — Assist, Tag, Counter and Smash. Managing this gauge is the game's core skill.
  • Link Attacks: pressing the same button repeatedly chains into auto-combo strings, keeping the entry barrier low while advanced players use manual routes.
Rule of thumb for new players: spend gauge on Assists and Counters first. Smash feels great, but losing your only escape option costs more rounds than it wins.

Tōkon uses a simplified attack scheme designed for newcomers, with depth layered on top.

Your First Hours: A Checklist

Follow this order and you'll reach ranked-ready fundamentals fast.

  1. Finish Episode Mode once — it doubles as the mechanics tutorial and pays out trophies.
  2. Pick one easy Point character (Spider-Man or Wolverine) and learn its Basic combo from our character guides.
  3. Practice calling Assists on reaction, not on panic — gauge discipline wins rounds.
  4. Learn one Wall Break route so you can unlock your 3rd fighter consistently.
  5. Play 10 online matches and note which opponent Assists trouble you most — then read that character's page.
