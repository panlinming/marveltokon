Best Team Comps & Assists Guide

Tōkon is won in team building: a good 4v4 squad covers its own weaknesses with Assists and Synergy. Below are three strong community compositions for launch version 1.0, plus how the Assist system actually works.

Version 1.0 · August 2026 4v4 Format

Top 3 Team Compositions

Each build pairs a Point fighter that opens rounds with Assist providers that cover its weaknesses.

Comp 1 · Meta Control

Spider-Man / Doctor Doom / Iron Man / Magik

Spider-Man (Point) Doctor Doom (Assist) Iron Man (Assist) Magik (Anchor)

Spider-Man opens with Web Zip mix-ups while Doom's Nullify Shield and Iron Man's Repulsor Shot assists lock down the screen. If the rush stalls, Magik closes rounds with her movement and counters. The safest all-around pick for ranked play.

Comp 2 · Rushdown Overload

Wolverine / Captain America / Black Panther / Ms. Marvel

Wolverine (Point) Captain America (Assist) Black Panther (Assist) Ms. Marvel (Anchor)

Four rushdown fighters that never stop pressing. Wolverine's Berserker pressure plus two aggressive assists force constant blocking, and the shared health bar means sustained chip pressure wins rounds even without big combos.

Comp 3 · Zoning Fortress

Iron Man / Storm / Peni Parker / Loki

Iron Man (Point) Storm (Assist) Peni Parker (Assist) Loki (Anchor)

The anti-rushdown answer: layers of projectiles make the screen nearly impossible to cross. Iron Man's Free Flight punishes desperate jumps, while Loki's tricks punish predictable approaches. Strong against Comp 2.

How Assists & Synergy Work

The Assemble Gauge is the resource behind every team interaction.

Assist Calls

Calling an Assist brings a teammate in for one action. Choose assists that cover your Point's weakness: projectile assists for rushdown characters, pressure assists for zoners.

Tag & Counter

The same gauge fuels defensive Tags and Counters. Spending everything on offense leaves you no escape when the opponent reverses the pressure.

Smash Activation

Smash consumes gauge for a burst action. In 1.0, Point-character strength weighs heavily because the shared health bar rewards whoever controls the neutral.

Synergy Pairings

Look for pairs where one fighter's strength erases the other's weakness — Spider-Man + Doom is the canonical example: Doom's shield removes the zoning that stops Spider-Man.
